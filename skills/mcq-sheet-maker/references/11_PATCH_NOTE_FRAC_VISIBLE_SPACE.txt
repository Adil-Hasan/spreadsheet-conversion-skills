Fraction Visible Space Patch
============================

Problem:
Raw trailing newline rules such as `\n` or `\n\n` may not show a visible blank line in Excel because generators/viewers may trim or visually ignore trailing blank lines.

New rule:
Cells containing `\frac` must end with:

\n\u00A0

That means:
- one newline
- then one non-breaking space
- at the very end of the cell value

This creates exactly one visible blank line under the text.

Also required:
- enable text wrapping
- vertical alignment top
- do not strip or trim final whitespace
